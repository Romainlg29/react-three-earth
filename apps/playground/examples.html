Examples

Each card opens a map focused on one feature, on phones too. They run on WebGPU (Safari and others fall back to WebGL 2) and stream real terrain, OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap tiles.

Start here

Showcase

Most features on one map: globe and flat map, stylised terrain, the sun, clusters, a heatmap, markers, 3D buildings, draw, measure, search and compare. Drop your own GeoJSON files on it.

Data and styles

3D buildings

New York, Hong Kong, Tokyo and Chicago from OpenStreetMap, standing on the terrain and tinted by height.

Labels in every script

Arabic and Hebrew right to left, Hindi and Thai clusters, Japanese in vertical columns, all drawn with the device's fonts.

GeoJSON, clusters and popups

Sixty summits from app data: clusters that split as you zoom, hover highlights and a popup per peak.

Compare

A street map and satellite imagery, or two years of satellite pictures, on either side of a slider. One globe, one camera, one render.

Draw

Points, lines and polygons drawn with a headless plugin, draped on the terrain and kept as GeoJSON.

Measure

Distances and areas, geodesic on the ellipsoid, with the terrain's elevation profile along a line. A headless plugin on the drawing one.

Shapes and solids

Circles, rectangles and spheres in two taps, extruded into cylinders, boxes and prisms on the terrain. GeoJSON with altitudes.

Search places

A headless search box: results as you type from Photon and the app's own GeoJSON, arrow keys and Enter, a flight to the place, and reverse geocoding on click.

Heatmap

The largest cities weighted by population, turning into labelled circles as you zoom in.

Plugins: glowing roads, neon names

A night map of Tokyo, Paris, New York and Dubai: a custom layer type glows around the major roads, a symbol effect draws neon names.

Image overlay

A picture pinned to four corners: a rain radar loop over the Low Countries, with an opacity slider.

Terrain and interaction

Terrain and relief

The Matterhorn, Everest and the Grand Canyon from elevation tiles, with colour relief, hillshade and an exaggeration slider.

A map in a scrolling page

Cooperative gestures: the page scrolls past the map; two fingers or Ctrl + wheel move it, and the keyboard works too.

Globe and playground

Globe

Terrain, imagery and the atmosphere around Mount Fuji. Use the button in the corner to morph to the flat map.

Flat map (2D)

The same scene starting in the 2D map view.

From space

The whole globe with its atmosphere; fly down by scrolling.

Sunset

Same place at dusk: the sun position comes from the date.

Markers and popups

Pins that follow the terrain, a controlled popup, a buried label and a draggable marker.

Plugin: SDF shapes

A raymarched signed-distance shape from an example plugin, half buried in the mountain.

Plugin: render passes

A vignette with a highlight lift, added as a plugin render pass.

No atmosphere

Plain lights instead of the physically based sky, the cheapest setup.

Afterburner

An aircraft effect sharing the Earth's atmosphere.
