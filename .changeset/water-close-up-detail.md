Water: close-up detail and sun glitter. The ripples finer than the smallest wave tile, the rest of Cox & Munk's slope variance from the wind, are drawn as normal detail near the camera (`WAVE_DETAIL_SCALES`; `WaveSurface.setSurfaceMeanSquareSlope`), and the roughness gives up what they draw. On the wave mesh, the sun's glint off the ripples too fine to draw glitters: cells about a pixel wide flash in turn along the glint, instead of one smooth, milky highlight.
